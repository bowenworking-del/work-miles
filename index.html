Common questions

Drives aren't being recorded. Make sure the switch at the top of the app is on, and that location access is set to "Always" in Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > Work Miles. Drives appear a few minutes after you park.

Why does a drive show "≈" next to the miles? Part of the drive is still being measured along the roads with Apple Maps. This finishes automatically when your phone has an internet connection.

How do I export my work miles? Open the Summary tab, pick the period, and tap "Export work log (CSV)".

Where is my data stored? Only on your iPhone. There's no account. See the privacy policy.
